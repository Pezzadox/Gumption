Questions people ask first

Can it spend money without me?

Only if you set a category to "just handle it", and never above your spending ceiling. Out of the box everything is "ask me first".

How do you know a saving is real?

We compare like for like on your metered usage, then check the first bill from the new deal. Until then it sits under estimated.

What can it see when I connect my email?

Bills, receipts, booking confirmations and renewal notices. You can review the list of matched emails and remove any of them.

What if a switch goes wrong?

Energy switches carry a 14-day cooling-off period. The activity log shows the days left and a cancel button. A person picks up anything the agent cannot resolve.

How do I leave?

Controls, then "Close account and erase data". It takes effect straight away.
